The project focused on a digital platform that connects the next generation of young entrepreneurs and business professionals with key figures in the Israeli economy.

## Design decisions

<Phones>
  <Figure phone file="calcalist-next/screen-home-get-connected.png" alt="Home before joining a conference: a large bubble with a plus and the label Get connected." />
  <Figure phone file="calcalist-next/screen-home-contacts.png" alt="New conference contacts shown as a cluster of profile bubbles." />
</Phones>

We chose the bubble as our core design motif because it embodies the mission: giving access to the “business bubble”. When users tap the plus on a bubble, they create a new connection and become part of that network, symbolically breaking through the barrier.

We chose blue because it represents technology: we wanted to show Calcalist's technological side.

## Brand values

- Prestigious
- Intelligent
- Technological
- Innovative
- Professional
- Reliable

## Identifying a need

In a discussion with Calcalist representatives, they emphasized the need for a platform that fosters mingling and networking during the company's conferences, while also providing a structured summary of the key content presented.

## Target audiences

- Key business and market leaders (senior executives in key positions)
- Business people (500–800 per conference)
- Entrepreneurs and investors
- Young people interested in connecting to the business world
- Speakers and domain experts
- Calcalist subscribers (~70,000)
- Website readers (3,000,000 unique monthly visitors)

## The solution

In response, we developed a design concept based on the “business bubble” motif, symbolizing the dynamics of professional interactions. The platform helps participants connect easily, share knowledge and build meaningful relationships, and makes key conference insights more accessible, so attendees keep benefiting from the knowledge and connections after the event.
