## Calcalist Next

Calcalist is one of Israel's leading business media outlets. As part of my studies, I took part in a project with Calcalist as the client: a digital platform that connects the next generation of entrepreneurs and business professionals with key figures in the Israeli economy.

## Identifying a need

In a discussion with Calcalist's representatives, they stressed the need for a platform that encourages mingling and networking during the company's conferences, and that gives a structured summary of the key content presented.

**The goal:** help participants connect easily and build meaningful relationships, and keep the conference's insights accessible after the event.

## Target audiences

One app had to work for very different people at once, from senior executives to students looking for a way into the business world. Figures are the client's.

- Key business and market leaders
- Entrepreneurs and investors
- Young people who want to connect to the business world
- Speakers and domain experts
- Calcalist subscribers and website readers
