## Calcalist Next

Calcalist is one of Israel's leading business media outlets, and it runs conferences that bring together entrepreneurs, investors and senior business figures. As part of my studies, our team designed Calcalist Next for Calcalist as a real client: a platform that helps attendees build professional connections at these conferences and keeps the event's key content within reach after it ends.

## Research

We worked from three sources:

- **A meeting with Calcalist.** One Calcalist representative worked directly with our team.
- **A questionnaire shared with the client.** [TODO: who answered it and how many responses there were]
- **Desk research and competitor analysis.** [TODO: one line on what the competitor analysis showed]

Two insights shaped the product. Both reflect the client's needs as Calcalist described them, not findings validated with attendees.

1. **Networking is hard in a busy room.** At a crowded conference, attendees need a fast way to find and connect with the right people while the event is happening.
2. **A conference's value fades when it ends.** Key content and new connections should stay accessible afterwards, so attendees keep benefiting from the event.

## Target audiences

One app had to work for very different people, from senior executives to young people looking for a way into the business world. Figures are the client's.

- Key business and market leaders
- Entrepreneurs and investors
- Young people who want to connect to the business world
- Speakers and domain experts
- Calcalist subscribers and website readers

## The concept: the business bubble

<Phones>
  <Figure phone file="calcalist-next/screen-home-get-connected.png" alt="Home before joining a conference: a large bubble with a plus and the label Get connected." caption="Get connected" />
  <Figure phone file="calcalist-next/screen-home-contacts.png" alt="New conference contacts shown as a cluster of profile bubbles above scheduled meetings." caption="Your network" />
</Phones>

Every connection is a bubble. The bubble stands for the networking concept itself, the “business bubble” attendees want to enter, and the plus on each bubble is the action that gets them in: tap it, and you create a new connection and join that person's network.

The same shape runs through the interface, from the empty home screen that invites you to get connected to the cluster of people you met at a conference, so the product's purpose and its visual identity tell one story.

## Key decisions

<Decisions>
  <Decision
    title="Badge scanning to connect"
    choice="Attendees connect by scanning each other's conference badge, instead of exchanging contact details by hand."
    why="Swapping details by hand slows people down in a busy event. Scanning takes seconds, which reduces the friction of reaching out and encourages spontaneous professional connections, the first need Calcalist raised."
  />
  <Decision
    title="Blue, not Calcalist red"
    choice="We gave Calcalist Next its own identity in deep blue, rather than following Calcalist's familiar red."
    why="A distinct color separates the new platform from the news brand and emphasizes its technological character. Calcalist's CEO specifically said she appreciated this choice."
  />
</Decisions>

## Results and feedback

The concept was presented to Calcalist. The review panel praised the project's high visual design quality, and Calcalist's CEO specifically praised the decision to use blue rather than the brand's familiar red.

## Reflection

This project taught me to translate a client's business needs into a cohesive digital product and visual language. Looking back, I would validate the proposed user flows with potential conference attendees earlier in the process, rather than relying mainly on the client's view of their needs.
