## Context

Calcalist is one of Israel's leading business media outlets. The brief was to develop a digital platform connecting the next generation of young entrepreneurs and business professionals with key figures in the Israeli economy.

## The problem

In a discussion with Calcalist representatives, they emphasized the need for a platform that fosters mingling and networking during the company's conferences, while also providing a structured summary of the key content presented.

## Goal

Help participants connect easily, share knowledge and build meaningful relationships, and keep key conference insights accessible so attendees keep benefiting after the event.

## Users

The platform serves several audiences at once, from senior executives to students who want a way into the business world. Figures are the client's.

## Solution

### Design concept: “Business Bubble”

The bubble is the core motif because it embodies the mission: giving access to the “business bubble”. When a user taps the plus on a bubble, they create a new connection and join that network, symbolically breaking through the barrier.
